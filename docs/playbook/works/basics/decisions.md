| basics-13 | 2026-11-21 | Every action has a weight, and the weight says whether the owner is asked. Light: a record that follows from a command the owner ran and that git undoes, such as the line "Last audit", a unit marked Done, a "Replaced by" mark at close, or a link the check flags; it is done without asking, since running the command is the yes. Medium: anything that states how the product is or how Claude behaves, such as resolving a contradiction, a new rule, a technology, or accepting a spec; it is asked once, and the answer is the decision, with no separate confirmation.
