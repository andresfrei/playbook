# Decisions of the work driven-mode

| ID | Date | Decision | Who decided | Their words |
|----|------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| driven-mode-1 | 2026-11-28 | Open the work `driven-mode`, left by dialog-learning-5. Whoever launches a session sets a variable of playbook's own (named by playbook, not by playbot, as basics-24 asks) that says the session is driven and names the session its questions go to. The session-start hook sees it and loads a short block in place of the dialog rules: ask in text with the type first, send the question to that session with SendMessage and end the turn, open no dialog, do not ask the language, and hand back what cannot be written outside the project. Without the variable nothing changes. Setting the variable in playbot's `launch.sh` is a work of the playbot repository. | Andres Frei | Shown the picture with the example `PLAYBOOK_DRIVEN_BY=playbot`: "es la idea" |
| driven-mode-2 | 2026-11-28 | The variable is `PLAYBOOK_DRIVEN_BY=<session name>`: its value is the session each question goes to with SendMessage, so the block does not depend on the launcher's prompt, which a compaction can summarize away, while the SessionStart hook runs again on `compact` (hooks.md: "`SessionStart` \| how the session started \| `startup`, `resume`, `clear`, `compact`, `fork`", read 2026-11-28). Chosen over a bare mark `PLAYBOOK_DRIVEN=1` with the destination left in the prompt. | Andres Frei | Chose "Nombre de sesión (Recomendado)" |
| driven-mode-3 | 2026-11-28 | The questions a driven session asks in text stay outside what `audit-learning` reads: the owner's answer is already kept from the dialog the driving session opens, counted under that session's folder; attributing it to the driven project is a work of the playbot repository. Chosen over having driven-mode keep each text question and its reply in the driven project. | Andres Frei | Chose "Fuera de alcance (Recomendado)" |
| driven-mode-4 | 2026-11-28 | Accept the spec of the work driven-mode, with the unit driven and its three proposals: the block printed in place of the line of ask-to-clear-2; a value with a newline or a double quote, or longer than 200 characters, ignored with one line that says so; and no instruction to ask the language in a driven session. | Andres Frei | Chose "Acepto (Recomendado)" |
