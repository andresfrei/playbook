# Open questions of the work delegation

| ID | Question | Recommendation | What it blocks |
|----|----------|----------------|----------------|
| delegation-Q5 | Where does a delegation live, and how is it revoked? | As a row of the work's or the project's `decisions.md`, scoped to one kind of question in that project; a later decision revokes it, and a "yes but" or a "no" to a delegated kind makes the agent propose revoking it. | Closing stage 1 |
| delegation-Q6 | How is a decision taken under a delegation recorded? | As a row like any other, "Who decided" being "the agent, by delegation-N", and the owner is told in one line. Changes the format of a wiki for that column. | Closing stage 1 |
