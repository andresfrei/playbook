# Definition of the work rule-promotion

What graduating an evidence rule means. Each item is Decided, Proposed, or Open.

## Graduation

- **Decided (rule-promotion-3).** A general evidence rule at level 3 or more graduates out of `plugins/playbook-agents/evidence-rules.md` into the skill or agent whose job it describes, as a fixed step there.
- **Decided (rule-promotion-7).** A candidate is a kind of `evidence-rules.md` at level 3 or more. On 2026-11-20 there are six: `missing-test`, `test-passes-when-broken`, `documentation-contradicts-code`, `instructions-contradict-each-other`, `decided-behaviour-changed-without-decision`, `unexpected-input-value-unhandled`.
- **Decided (rule-promotion-7).** The destination is the one skill or agent whose job the rule's action belongs to, such as `review-change` or the agent `write`. `rules.md` is a destination only when the rule replaces text there, since it loads 620 of 620 words (L4).
- **Decided (rule-promotion-4).** The owner decides each graduation at the close of a work, next to consolidation: the agent proposes the exact text and its destination. Never delegated.
- **Decided (rule-promotion-5).** A graduated rule leaves `evidence-rules.md`; its row in `evidence.md` says "graduated to <path>". New findings of the kind are still stored; reaching the threshold again proposes revising the graduated step.
- **Decided (rule-promotion-6).** A graduated step comes with one case of behaviour in its skill or agent's evaluation cases, run only when the owner asks; the count of rule-promotion-5 shows afterwards whether it is enough.
