# playbook

Read `docs/wiki/README.md` before anything else. It is the single entry point: it says what this repository is, where work stands, and what comes next.

- The wiki is the source of truth. The definitions in force are in its shared pages, each citing its decision. Each work records its decisions in its own `decisions.md`; `docs/wiki/works/history/decisions.md` (`D#`, `TD#`) is the history from before works.
- Every statement is Decided, Proposed, or Open. Only the owner turns Proposed into Decided; record each decision with the owner's words verbatim.
- Write the wiki in English. Talk to the owner in their language.
- "Phase" means one of the product phases (D30, D104). "Stage" means one of the six method stages (D8). Do not mix them.
- Do not copy or rewrite any part of gentle-ai before phase 2 (D30, D31).
- Do not install, move, or delete skills on the owner's machine until the owner asks for that step.
- When a work closes, recommend whether it needs a new version and which one, MAJOR, MINOR, or PATCH by TD22, and ask the owner. On a yes, after the merge: the version in the three manifests, the tag, and the release on GitHub (github-release-2).

Read `docs/wiki/README.md` first. Before you change code, read the page that its page table names for the paths you change.
